JoyBot Inc. Partners with AWS to Introduce JoyAd Virtual Billboard Solutions, Powered by AWS

February 24, 2025 – Palo Alto, California — JoyBot Inc., a leading innovator in AI-driven video solutions, today introduced its new JoyAd Virtual Billboard Solution, powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS). This announcement follows JoyBot’s successful completion of AWS’s Foundational Technical Review (FTR), ensuring the solution meets AWS’s stringent technical, operational, and security standards.

JoyAd is a cutting-edge virtual insertion solution that leverages unused or blank spaces within video content to create dynamic, virtual billboards. These billboards can host tailored advertisements, video overlays, and interactive elements, offering content owners new incremental monetization opportunities while enhancing viewer experiences.

Leveraging AWS’s infrastructure, JoyAd also introduces advanced capabilities, including real-time 3D modeling, real-time rendering, and real-time insertion. The solution operates seamlessly based on pure streaming signals, without requiring additional on-site hardware or changes to internal operational processes. This streamlined approach allows JoyBot to scale rapidly and deliver significant value to all stakeholders in the content ecosystem.

“We’re excited to bring the JoyAd solution to life on a global scale. With AWS’s trusted infrastructure and the successful completion of the FTR, we can deliver a secure and dynamic platform that empowers content owners to unlock new revenue streams from unused video spaces, transforming how video monetization is approached.”

— Tong Zhang, CEO of JoyBot Inc.

Key Benefits of the JoyAd Virtual Billboard Solution

  • Incremental Revenue Streams: Unlock monetization opportunities by utilizing unused video space.
  • Real-Time Innovation: Enable real-time 3D modeling, rendering, and insertion based on streaming signals, without additional hardware or process changes.
  • Dynamic Ad Control: Customize and manage billboard content in real time across regions.
  • Global Reach: Scale deployments rapidly with AWS’s robust global network.
  • Enhanced Viewer Experience: Seamlessly integrate virtual billboards without disrupting video quality.

JoyAd Virtual Billboard is JoyBot’s latest advancement in its work with AWS.

About JOYX

JOYX (formerly JoyBot Inc.) is a cutting-edge spatial computing and AI video advertising company with deep R&D roots in Silicon Valley. Built on a cloud-native architecture running entirely on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and designed for the modern streaming era, JOYX’s proprietary AI platform utilizes real-time computer vision and 3D rendering to seamlessly integrate broadcast-grade virtual graphics directly into live video streams and venue environments. By transforming underutilized spaces into dynamic virtual billboards without physical on-site integrations, JOYX empowers broadcasters, leagues, and content owners to effortlessly elevate their visual presentation and maximize commercial revenue. https://myjoyx.com
