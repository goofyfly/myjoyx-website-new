The platform

JoyAd turns blank space into branded value.

JoyAd is a virtual insertion engine that finds the unused surfaces inside live video and fills them with broadcast-grade billboards — dynamic, regional, and seamless. New inventory, no new ad breaks.

Spatial intelligence

A spatial-AI engine trained on live sport.

Our self-trained spatial models understand the geometry, motion, and surfaces of live competition — adapting in real time, frame by frame. From a single broadcast feed to enhanced output, the entire pipeline runs in the cloud.

Ingest

Take the pure broadcast stream. Nothing added on-site — no cameras, sensors, or rigs.

Understand

Spatial AI builds a live 3D model of the scene and detects every insertable surface.

Render

Virtual billboards are generated and rendered to match lighting, motion, and depth.

Deliver

The enhanced stream goes out in real time — frame-accurate, seamless, broadcast-grade.
